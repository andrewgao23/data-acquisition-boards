In Altium, I checked where component groups routed to and made a list:
<br><br>
<img width="1335" height="755" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/53de3102-8a27-4746-a044-a03647979db3" />

Routing:
- analog filtering inputs from right connectors, goes to ADC
- ADC is on bottom right of MCU
- all CANs is isolated, does not need to connect to anything
- I2C 4, 1 goes to left connector
- I2C 3, 2, goes to right connector
- SPI 1 goes to left connector
- SPI 2 goes to right connector
- HSE,LSE CLK goes to left of MCU
- board ID jumper on left of MCU
- NVM chip on left/bottom of MCU
- thermistor filtering inputs from left connector, goes to bottom of MCU
- thermistor connects to left of MCU
- connector 4 goes to top right of MCU
<br><br>
In retrospect, this list was wrong because there were net naming issues on the CAN and I2C blocks, which would be addressed later.

**Feedback**
- No ground traces/vias needed due to ground pours
- Move thermistors to LDOs
- Match CAN lengths and make placement symmetric
- Space tightly spaced traces apart to prevent crosstalk
- Re-route bucks and LDOs according to datasheets to minimize inductive loops
- Re-route branches/acute angles on traces
- Space traces away from vias
- Place clock oscillators near MCU pins
